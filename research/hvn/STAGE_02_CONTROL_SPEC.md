# Stage 2 Non-HVN Control and Matching Specification

Status: **LOCKED BEFORE EMPIRICAL RESULTS**

Amended before empirical access by `STAGE_02_SPECIFICATION_AMENDMENT_01`.

## Candidate enumeration

Controls use only the same frozen profile as the treated HVN. For a treated
node of width `w` bins, enumerate every contiguous `w`-bin window within the
full materialized frozen-profile grid, including zero-weight bins.

A candidate is excluded if it:

- overlaps a qualifying HVN under the same configuration;
- touches a qualifying HVN boundary;
- contains the POC bin;
- leaves the materialized grid;
- has missing, duplicate, noncontiguous, or otherwise invalid bin data.

For half-open zones, “touches” includes candidate high equal to HVN low and
candidate low equal to HVN high. Thus at least one full profile bin separates a
control from every qualifying HVN. Controls always preserve exact treated
width in bins, though point width follows that profile’s common bin size.

Every candidate receives a stable ID based on profile, configuration, treated
node width, and start bin. Candidate construction never uses interaction or
forward behavior.

## C01 — neutral non-HVN

C01 candidates satisfy all general exclusions and have mean constituent-bin
weight inclusively between the profile’s 25th and 75th bin-weight percentiles.
Percentiles use exact type-7 linear interpolation over all materialized bin
weights. This is an ordinary non-HVN area.

## C02 — profile-mass-matched non-HVN

C02 candidates satisfy all general exclusions, do not contain the
representative peak bin of any qualifying local-prominence candidate, and are
not identical to any qualifying node zone. Their total zone-weight share is
compared with the treated node share:

```text
absolute proportional difference =
abs(control_share - treated_share) / treated_share
```

The denominator must be positive. The caliper is at most `0.20`. Candidates
are ranked by proportional difference, then absolute share difference, then
start bin and control-zone ID. No caliper relaxation is allowed. C02 isolates
local prominence beyond raw profile mass.

## Complete control ledgers

The control-opportunity ledger retains every valid C01 and C02 zone for every
relevant treated width/configuration, including untouched zones. Duplicate
zone definitions generated by multiple treated nodes of the same width are
represented once per profile/configuration/control family, with eligible
treated-node links preserved separately.

Each control opportunity parallels the HVN opportunity schema and adds at
least:

```text
control_zone_id, control_family, start_bin_index, end_bin_index,
mean_bin_weight, zone_weight, zone_weight_share, neutral_percentile_eligible,
mass_proportional_difference, locally_prominent, contains_poc,
touches_or_overlaps_hvn, eligible_treated_node_ids
```

First touch, classification, features, censoring, and forward metrics use the
identical event specification as HVNs. Complete control opportunity and event
ledgers are saved; matching is not a retention filter.

## Primary cross-session matching

Primary matching is run independently for C01 and C02 and exactly within:

```text
relationship
allocation method
bin ratio
prominence threshold
data year
approach side
zone width in bins
```

A pair must use different interaction-session dates. It requires two genuine
approach events, complete outcomes, and available matching features.
`START_INSIDE` is excluded. A control cannot be reused within a
relationship/method/ratio/prominence/year/control-family lane.

Treated events are processed in stable order:

```text
year, interaction date, touch time, profile_id, node_id, event_id
```

The fixed distance is:

```text
D =
1.0 * abs(touch-time difference minutes) / 60
+ 1.0 * abs(absolute 15m pre-touch displacement difference in ATR)
+ 1.0 * abs(15m pre-touch path-efficiency difference)
+ 0.5 * abs(interaction-open-distance difference in ATR)
+ 0.5 * abs(POC-distance difference in ATR)
+ 1.0 * abs(ATR-at-touch proportional difference)
```

Calipers are:

- interaction-window minute difference at most 60 minutes;
- absolute pre-touch displacement difference at most `0.75 ATR`;
- interaction-open-distance difference at most `1.00 ATR`;
- ATR-at-touch proportional difference at most `0.20`.

The lowest distance wins; `control_event_id` is the final tie-break. No
forward metric, touch outcome after event confirmation, or other
post-treatment feature enters eligibility, ranking, or calipers. No pairwise
forward-window overlap check is imposed because primary dates differ. Complete
15/30/60/120-minute outcomes remain mandatory.

## Secondary same-session matching

Secondary matching is separately labelled and descriptive. It requires the
same source profile, interaction session, relationship, method, ratio,
prominence, width, and approach side, with interaction-window minute
difference at most 60 and no control reuse.

It uses the original distance without the ATR term. Treated/control zones must
not overlap or touch, and definition duplicates in one economic episode cannot
pair. Overlapping forward windows are permitted; overlap is reported at
15/30/60/120 minutes and results are separated by overlap status where
observations exist. Secondary results cannot enter G01–G08.

## Match quality

Every lane reports treated events, available controls, pairs, match rate,
unmatched reasons, reuse count, touch-time, ATR, displacement, path-efficiency,
interaction-open, and POC difference distributions, caliper failures, and
pre/post balance for every matching covariate.

For a covariate:

```text
SMD = (mean_treated - mean_control) /
      sqrt((sample_variance_treated + sample_variance_control) / 2)
```

SMD is zero if the denominator and mean difference are both zero; otherwise a
zero denominator yields signed infinity. Absolute SMD over `0.20` is material
imbalance. A relationship cannot claim a causal effect while a major
unresolved post-match imbalance remains. Control reuse must equal zero.

The match ledger records lane, pair ID, treated/control IDs, all restrictions,
component distances, total distance, primary/secondary label, stable ordering,
and code/configuration hashes.

## Economic episodes

Definition-level HVN events are clustered when all conditions hold:

- same contract;
- same relationship;
- same interaction session;
- absolute touch-time difference at most five minutes;
- zone intersection width divided by the narrower zone width is at least
  `0.50`;
- same approach side.

Clustering is deterministic connected-component clustering: events are sorted
by contract, relationship, session, touch time, zone bounds, and event ID;
qualifying links form undirected components. A transitive component receives a
content-derived `economic_episode_id`.

The ledger reports raw definition events, unique episodes, definitions per
episode, and represented methods, ratios, and prominence thresholds. Events
never cluster across contracts. Configuration-level inference retains its own
events. Cross-configuration summaries use episodes and do not treat duplicate
definitions as independent.
